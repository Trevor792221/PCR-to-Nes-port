**Pixel Racer NES**: an unofficial NES demake of the drag-racing and
car-building style of Pixel Car Racer. All code, art, music and car names
are original.

### Downloads
* **`pxracer.nes`**: the game. Run it in an NES emulator or copy it to a
  flash cart (EverDrive N8, PowerPak and similar). MMC1 / SNROM with a
  battery save. Timing is tuned for NTSC.
* **`pixel-racer-soundtrack.zip`**: the original soundtrack as WAV files.
  The three tracks are also attached individually.

### Controls
A = gas (hold on the line to rev), Right = shift up / launch on green,
Left = shift down, B = nitrous, Up/Down = change lane in street races,
Start = pause.
